Mid-level donors are one of the most resilient segments available to forward-thinking organizations. However, despite their reliable commitment, this group often falls into a communication gap. When mid-range givers receive neither the high-touch cultivation reserved for major benefactors nor the personalized engagement needed to sustain their support, significant revenue is left on the table.
