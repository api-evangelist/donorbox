Artificial intelligence (AI) is reshaping the nonprofit sector. Whether it’s used to curate content or analyze fundraising data, AI can significantly streamline your workload and free up more time for mission-focused work. Even so, studies show that only 24% of nonprofits have an AI policy in place.
